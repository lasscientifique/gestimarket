# Owner space, subscription and licence

## Owner space (Android app)

With the shop's Eden Dev account (e-mail and password), on any phone:

- **My shops**: each shop, its **server on or off**, its plan and end date. The list stays readable without Internet.
- **Open** a shop remotely: the app screen opens, only the data goes over the Internet. Then sign in with your username and PIN. “Change shop” goes back to the list.
- **Subscriptions**: the latest payments, activation with a **voucher card**, payment with **Orange Money** or **Moov Money** (reference received by SMS).
- **Details** (under each shop): the owner's full name, **phone**, city and address, saved with Eden Dev. The shop name, country and type do not change: ask Eden Dev.
- **Contact Eden Dev**: WhatsApp, call, e-mail.

The session stays open on the phone; “Sign out” erases it.

## Shop account: address, e-mail, password

- **Confirm the address**: when the account is created, a **6-digit code** is sent to the chosen e-mail. Type it to finish: the shop is created at that moment. Not received? Check the spam folder, then “Send the code again”. If the window was closed, the code is asked for at the next sign-in.
- **Change the e-mail**: Owner space → “My account” → “Change the account e-mail”, or Subscription & licence → “Change the account e-mail”. Type the password and the new address, then the **code** received at the new address. This is also the fix when the address was mistyped at sign-up.
- **Forgotten password**: “Forgot your password?” (Owner space, Subscription & licence, or “Forgot the administrator PIN”). Type the account e-mail: a **code** is sent there. Type that code and the new password.
- **Change the password**: Owner space → “My account” → “Change the password”, or Subscription & licence → “Change the account password”. The current password is asked for.
- A good password has **at least 8 characters, with letters and digits**. 12345678, “azerty123” or the shop name are refused: this account opens the shop remotely and pays the subscription.
- On the PC, **Subscription & licence → “Shop details”** also changes the phone and address (the account password is asked for).

## Subscription and licence (shop PC)

Subscription & licence shows the plan, the end date and the stations in use. To extend:

- **Voucher card**: code printed on the card bought from a reseller, the subscription is activated right away;
- **Orange Money**: pay the merchant code, then enter the reference received by SMS; Eden Dev validates;
- **Visa card**: secure payment page.

Without an account, the shop runs for **14 days as a free trial** (PRO plan, 3 stations): Subscription & licence shows the days left and offers to create the account.

The licence is checked **without Internet** on the station. Reminders 7, 3 and 1 days before the end; after the end, GestiMarket becomes **read-only**: the data stays viewable, sales are blocked.
