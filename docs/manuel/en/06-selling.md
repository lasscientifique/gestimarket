# Selling at the till

## The cashier's day

1. **Open the till**: Till sessions → Open, declaring the opening float.
2. **Serve** each customer (below).
3. **Close the till** in the evening: count the cash, GestiMarket computes the difference. The day's Z report is numbered and can no longer be changed.

## Serving a customer

1. **Scan** the items: barcode scanner, phone camera (camera button), or tap the tiles. Type `3*code` to add 3 items at once.
2. **Customer** (optional): F4. Required for a sale on the customer account.
3. **Take payment**: F9. Choose the payment method. “Partial payment” combines several methods (cash + Orange Money…).
4. **Receipt**: print it, then “New sale”.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| F2 | Search a product |
| F4 | Choose the customer |
| F8 | Tickets on hold |
| F9 or F12 | Take payment |
| Ctrl + K | Where is this product? (everywhere) |

## Mobile payments

Orange Money, Moov Money, Sarali, Wave… are set up in Settings → Payments. The merchant QR code is shown on the customer display: the customer scans and pays, the cashier enters the reference received by SMS.

## Discounts and controls

- A discount above the threshold (5% by default) is approved with a **manager's PIN**.
- Removing an item or clearing the ticket can require a PIN (Settings → Security).
- The same ticket sent twice (network drop) is recorded only once.

## Other tills

The “Other tills” button shows colleagues' tickets live. A customer can be **taken over** on another till (with the PIN).

## Returns and credit notes

Sales history or Returns & credit notes: choose the sale, the returned items, then the refund: cash, **credit note** (numbered voucher, payable at the till) or on the customer's account. A damaged item is not put back in stock.
