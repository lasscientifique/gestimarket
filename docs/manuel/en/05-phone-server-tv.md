# Shop without a computer: the phone server and the TV

An Android phone can keep the whole shop and share it with a TV (customer display) and other tills, **without a computer and even without Internet**. The PRO plan is needed to connect other devices.

## 1. Preparing the phone server

1. Install GestiMarket, choose **My shop on this phone**, then create the shop.
2. Open **Station & network**, choose **Shop server**, then **Save and restart**.
3. Android shows a “GestiMarket: shop server active” notification: the server keeps running even with the **screen locked**.
4. Tap **Allow in background** if the button appears: otherwise Android's battery saver may stop the server.
5. Keep the phone **on its charger** during opening hours.

## 2. Turning on the hotspot

Tap **Open hotspot settings** (Station & network), then turn on the **mobile hotspot**. It works **without a data plan**: the devices talk to each other.

On the other devices, connect the Wi-Fi to this hotspot. If Android says “No Internet access”, choose **Stay connected**.

## 3. The Android TV (customer display)

1. Install the same app on the TV and open it from the TV home screen.
2. The TV **finds the shop by itself**: choose it with the remote.
3. Choose the **till** to show (“Caisse 1”…).
4. The TV shows the scanned items, the total, the Orange Money QR code and the promotions. The screen stays on and **reconnects by itself** after an interruption.

To change shop or till: the small button at the top of the customer display (remote).

## 4. Other tills

On another phone or tablet: **Join a shop**, then tap the shop found. On an iPhone: scan the server's QR code with the Camera.

## 5. Protecting the data

A phone can be lost or broken: GestiMarket makes an automatic copy every day, but **inside the phone**. Every week, a reminder asks you to **send a copy elsewhere**: Settings → Backup → **Save a copy…**, then choose WhatsApp, Google Drive or a computer.
