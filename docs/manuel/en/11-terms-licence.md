# Terms of use, licence and help

## Accepting the terms

At first start, before creating the shop, GestiMarket shows three texts:

- the **terms of use**: use of the software, account and password, staff, the merchant's obligations, backups;
- the **licence agreement**: what each plan allows, trial, payment, end of subscription, what is forbidden;
- the **personal data policy**: what stays in the shop (sales, customers, debts) and what Eden Dev receives (account, shop, payments).

Each is summarised, with “Read in full”. Tick the box, then “Accept and continue”. The accepted version, the date and the administrator's name are recorded in the shop. The Windows installer also shows the licence agreement.

When an update changes these texts, the administrator accepts them again at sign-in. Staff have nothing to do.

## Reading them again

**Settings → Help, terms and licence** shows the three texts, the accepted version, its date and who accepted it. They are also in the appendix of this manual. In English, the French version prevails.

## The manual and videos

- **Settings → Help, terms and licence → Read the manual**: this manual opens inside GestiMarket, page by page, even without Internet. “Open in a PDF reader” opens it in the device's reader, to print or send it.
- Each screen's **Help** button explains the screen and offers its videos.
- **Offline help** (same tab): download the videos once (about 25 MB); they then work without Internet, here and on the devices linked to this server station.
