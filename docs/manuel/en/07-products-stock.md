# Products, stock and shop plan

## Products

- **Add**: Products → New product: name, category, purchase and sale price, stock, alert threshold, photo. A barcode is created automatically if the product has none.
- **Packagings** (carton, pack) and **wholesale prices** by quantity or for wholesale customers.
- **Import** a catalogue from Excel (CSV): Products → Import.
- **Labels** for shelves and products with barcodes: select the products, then Labels.

## Stock

- **Alerts**: products under the threshold or out of stock, and products expiring soon (Stock & inventory, dashboard).
- **Adjust**: loss, breakage, inventory. Each movement is recorded with its author.
- **Purchases**: Purchases / deliveries → new supplier delivery. Stock goes up and the average purchase price (WAC) is recomputed.

## Shop plan

Shop plan: place the fixtures (gondolas, fridges, displays…) at their real size, on one or several floors, then store the products on their **shelves** and **slots**. The plan is shown from above or in **3D**, with the stock state or the sales of each fixture.

## Locating a product

**Ctrl + K** or Locate a product: type the name or scan the barcode.

- **Top**: the fixture blinks on the plan.
- **3D and path**: the shop in 3D, with the path from the entrance.
- **Visit**: the camera starts at the entrance, at eye level, and **walks by itself** to the fixture, then shows the shelf and slot. Pause, restart, ×2 speed, “Look around” to walk freely, full screen on phones.
