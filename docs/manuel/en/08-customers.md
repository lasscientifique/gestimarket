# Customers: account, debts, funds and loyalty

## The customer account

Each customer has **a single balance**:

- in **red**, the customer **owes** money to the shop (debt);
- in **green**, the customer has **deposited funds** at the shop.

**Deposit** (Customers → Deposit): the money first repays the debt, the rest becomes funds.

**Paying with the account**: at the till, choose “Customer account”. The funds are used first, then the rest becomes a debt, within the customer's **credit limit** (0 = no credit).

**Funds refunded** to the customer: with a manager's PIN.

The customer's **statement** shows purchases, deposits, refunded funds and returns.

## Loyalty programme (PRO and BUSINESS plans)

Turn it on in Settings → Sales → Loyal customer.

- **0 to 5 stars** depending on the number of purchases in the last 12 months (adjustable steps: 3, 10, 25, 50, 100 by default).
- A **bonus** of 0.5% to 1% of the amount paid on each purchase, depending on the stars.
- The bonus **pays all or part** of a purchase at the till (“Loyalty bonus”).
- The bonus expires after 12 months without a purchase (adjustable).
- Printable **loyalty card** with QR code: scanned at the till, it selects the customer.
