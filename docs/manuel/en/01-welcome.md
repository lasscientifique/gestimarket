# Welcome to GestiMarket

GestiMarket is the point-of-sale and management software for shops, mini-markets, supermarkets, wholesalers, pharmacies, hardware stores and every kind of business. It is built for African shops: it works **without Internet**, **without a computer** if needed, and accepts mobile payments (Orange Money, Moov Money, Sarali, Wave…).

## What GestiMarket does

- **Till**: scan items (barcode scanner or phone camera), discounts, wholesale prices, promotions, payment in cash, mobile money, card, customer account or loyalty bonus, printed receipt.
- **Products and stock**: product sheets with photo and barcode, categories, locations in the shop, out-of-stock and expiry alerts, inventory, supplier purchases.
- **Customers**: customer account (debts and deposited funds), loyalty programme (stars and bonus), loyalty card with QR code.
- **Shop plan** in 2D and 3D, with the “Where is this product?” search and the guided visit to the shelf.
- **Management**: dashboard, reports, till sessions, activity log, backups.
- **Several devices**: PCs, phones, tablets, Android TVs (customer display), connected over Wi-Fi or a phone's hotspot.

## On which devices?

| Device | How | Possible role |
|---|---|---|
| Windows PC | Installer or portable version | Single station, shop server, till |
| Android phone or tablet | App (APK) | Shop on the phone, shop server, till, owner space |
| Android TV | The same app | Customer display of a till |
| iPhone, iPad | Safari (“Add to Home Screen”) | Till or viewing, connected to the server |

## Plans

| Plan | For whom | What it allows |
|---|---|---|
| **FREE** | Trial, expired subscription | Viewing only (read-only) |
| **STARTER** | Small shop, one device | Sales, stock, customers on 1 station |
| **PRO** | Shop with several devices | 3 stations, shop server (PC or phone), Internet access, customer display (TV), logo, loyalty programme |
| **BUSINESS** | Large store | 10 stations, everything in PRO |

A new installation starts with a **14-day trial of the PRO plan**. The subscription is paid from GestiMarket: voucher card, Orange Money, Moov Money or Visa card (see “Subscription and licence”).

## Your data stays with you

Sales, products, stock and customers are stored **in the shop** (on the server PC or phone), never at Eden Dev. Only the shop account (e-mail, name, country, city) and the licence go over the Internet.
