## 1. Purpose

These terms govern the use of the GestiMarket software (application for computer, phone, tablet and TV) and of the Eden Dev online services that come with it: shop account, licence, subscription payment, shop Internet address, help.

## 2. Acceptance

Using GestiMarket requires accepting these terms, the licence agreement and the personal data policy. The shop administrator accepts them at first start, on behalf of the shop and its staff. The accepted version, its date and the administrator's name are recorded in the shop.

## 3. Shop account

- The account is created by the shop owner, or by a person they authorise, with accurate information.
- The owner keeps the password secret and chooses a password of at least 8 characters, with letters and digits.
- This account opens the shop remotely and pays the subscription: an action made with it is deemed made by the owner.
- If the password is lost or stolen, the owner changes it at once (“Forgot your password”) and informs Eden Dev.

## 4. Shop staff

The administrator creates the staff accounts (username and PIN code) and decides their rights, and is responsible for how the staff use the software. Each PIN code is personal and must not be lent.

## 5. Permitted use

GestiMarket is for managing a lawful business: sales, stock, purchases, customers, till. It must not be used:

- for an illegal activity;
- to record data unrelated to the shop;
- to mislead customers (false receipts, false discounts);
- to disrupt Eden Dev's services.

## 6. Merchant's obligations

The merchant alone remains responsible for:

- their tax and accounting obligations (returns, receipts, invoices, VAT);
- the prices, stock and amounts they enter;
- the information they record about their customers (see the personal data policy).

GestiMarket is a management tool: it does not replace an accountant, nor a certified till software where the law requires one.

## 7. Backups

- The shop's data is stored on its own devices, not at Eden Dev.
- GestiMarket makes an automatic copy every day, lets you make others (protected by a password or not) and restore them.
- The merchant must regularly keep a copy off the device: USB stick, Google Drive, another phone.
- Eden Dev cannot recover data lost without a backup, nor open a protected backup whose password is lost.

## 8. Online services

- The account, licence, payment and shop Internet address go through Eden Dev's providers (Supabase, Cloudflare, GitHub).
- Eden Dev does its best to keep them available, without being able to guarantee it at all times.
- Without Internet, GestiMarket keeps working on the shop's devices: the licence is checked on the device.

## 9. Updates

Eden Dev publishes updates that fix and improve the software; the merchant is encouraged to install them. A version that is too old may no longer communicate with the online services.

## 10. Support

Support is provided by WhatsApp, phone and e-mail, during Bamako business hours. It may ask for the station's log (Settings → Eden Dev developer), which contains neither sales nor customers.

## 11. Suspension

In case of serious breach (fraud, licence circumvention, unlawful use, non-payment), Eden Dev may suspend the licence after warning the owner. The shop's data stays viewable read-only and is never deleted by Eden Dev.
