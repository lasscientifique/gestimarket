# Personal data

Version 2026-11 · Publisher: Eden Dev, Bamako (Mali) · WhatsApp +223 97 12 62 02 · edendev.gestimarket@gmail.com · Translation: the French version prevails.

## 1. Who is responsible for what

- **The shop** is responsible for the data it records in GestiMarket: customers, debts, sales, staff. This data stays on its devices; Eden Dev has no access to it.
- **Eden Dev** is responsible only for the data sent to its online services (point 2).

## 2. Data received by Eden Dev, and why

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Account e-mail and password (the password is kept encrypted by Supabase; Eden Dev cannot read it) | Sign-in, password recovery |
| Shop: name, owner's name, phone, country, city, address, type of business | Licence, billing, support |
| Device identifier (computed, no personal data), PC name, GestiMarket version | Link the licence to the device, know the installed version |
| Payments: plan, amount, method, transaction reference, payer's number | Activate and follow the subscription |
| Account WhatsApp number, if chosen | Sign-in, sending codes on WhatsApp |
| WhatsApp messages sent to GestiMarket's number: number and time of the last message, GestiMarket messages not delivered (never the message text) | Send a code only if the person wrote within 24 hours, as WhatsApp requires |
| Version of the terms accepted when the account was created | Proof of acceptance |
| Server station address (if the mobile app or the Internet address is enabled) | Open the shop remotely |
| Crash reports: error message, screen, device type, version, without any business data (can be turned off: Settings → Security) | Fix errors |

Eden Dev never receives sales, products, stock, customers, debts or PIN codes.

## 3. Where this data is

At Supabase, in Ireland (European Union). Traffic to the shop's Internet address goes through Cloudflare; help videos and updates are downloaded from GitHub. Codes sent on WhatsApp go through Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform). These providers apply their own security rules.

## 4. How long

Account and payment data is kept while the account exists, then for the period required by law for accounting records. Crash reports are kept for 12 months. WhatsApp message records (time of the last message, messages not delivered) are erased 30 days after the last activity.

## 5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your account data, by WhatsApp (+223 97 12 62 02) or by e-mail (edendev.gestimarket@gmail.com). Deleting the account ends the licence; the shop's data stays on your devices. You can also contact Mali's personal data protection authority (APDP).

## 6. Your customers' data

If you record your customers' names, phone numbers or debts:

- tell them about it and why (credit, loyalty);
- record only what is necessary;
- correct or remove their data when they ask (customer record, then archive).

## 7. Security

- PIN codes are kept encrypted.
- Backups can be protected by a password.
- The shop's Internet address uses HTTPS.
- Every important action is logged in the activity log.

On the shop network, use a password-protected Wi-Fi: exchanges between the tills and the shop server are not encrypted there yet.
